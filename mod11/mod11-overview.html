Module 11 Overview

Data Structure Derby

Welcome to the Derby

All right, data structures aficionados: it is time to don your racing helmets and place your bets. Welcome to the Data Structure Derby, where hash tables, binary search trees, vectors, and linked lists compete head-to-head in a penultimate performance showdown.

This capstone active learning assignment asks you to put your Big-O expectations to the test. Rather than relying only on theory, you will gather real benchmark data and compare how these structures behave in practice.

What You Will Do

Predict Winners

Start by making informed predictions about which structures should excel on particular operations.

Run Benchmarks

Collect timing data by running controlled experiments on multiple data structures.

Generate Plots

Turn raw measurements into visual evidence that makes performance trends easy to compare.

Analyze Results

Explain where the data matches your expectations and where reality turns out to be more interesting.

⚠ This capstone active learning assignment counts for half of your active learning credit for the course, so plan your time and effort accordingly.

The Contenders

Hash Tables

Will O(1) average-case lookup let them sprint to the finish line?

Binary Search Trees

Can they stay balanced and composed enough to remain competitive?

Vectors

Will cache-friendly layout and low constant factors produce an upset?

Linked Lists

Can pointer-based flexibility overcome their poor locality and linear search?

May the best data structure, or structures, win.
